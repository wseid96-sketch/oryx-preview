About

A Lower Mainland freight company, built one van at a time.

Oryx Mainland Logistics is the cargo-van freight and delivery service of ORYX MAINLAND HOLDINGS INC., a British Columbia company based in Surrey.

The approach

Start small. Run it properly. Grow when it's earned.

Phase one is a single cargo van working the Lower Mainland, and doing it right: legal and compliant, safe, documented on every load, and priced so the work is sustainable.

We'd rather do fewer jobs well than chase every load. Repeat customers who trust the van to show up are the goal, not a busy calendar. More vans come later, once the first one is running the way it should.
